Sebenzanathi Employee Management System

An employee management system for small and medium businesses. It brings attendance, tasks, and team communication into one place, for the office and the warehouse floor.

Explore features

Everything you need to manage the team

  • Smart attendance

    Replace paper clock-in with a mobile app. Overtime and sick leave are requested and handled as they happen.

  • Task management

    Assign work, set a priority, and see progress. Busy hours do not depend on someone remembering the list.

  • Team communication

    Chat lives in the system, so a warehouse conversation does not disappear into a personal messaging app.

  • Wages

    Pay is worked out from the hours that were actually recorded, and a digital payslip can be produced from that.

  • Reporting

    See how the workforce is doing, and pull a company summary instead of rebuilding it from paper.

  • Access by role

    Managers and employees see what their role allows. The web office and the phone talk to the same system.

Why we built Sebenzanathi

Businesses such as MTM Distributors still run attendance on paper. Records go missing, and pay is worked out from numbers nobody fully trusts.

On a large floor, distance breaks communication. Tasks get forgotten, or they get ordered by whoever remembers them first.

Sebenzanathi puts attendance, work, messages, leave, and pay in one system the office and the floor can both open.

How it is built

  • Web React
  • Phone React Native
  • API ASP.NET Core
  • Data PostgreSQL
